This page answers some of the questions owners ask most often when they're considering working with a property management company in Chania.

Offered Services

Essential questions to understand who we are, what we do, and who we work with.

Minos Host provides management services for villas, holiday homes and short-term rentals in Chania. Depending on the partnership, we can take on guest communication, booking coordination, check-in, cleaning, maintenance follow-up and keeping the owner informed.

We work with owners of villas, apartments, holiday homes and short-term rentals who need dependable day-to-day support.

Minos Host focuses on Chania and selected nearby areas, where we can offer real local support. We don't take on areas where we can't guarantee proper coordination and consistent service.

Online Presence

How we help manage bookings, reviews, and your presence across key platforms.

Yes. We can help manage and improve the property's presence on Airbnb and Booking.com. Support can include listing updates, account coordination, better presentation and keeping information consistent across platforms.

Yes. We can monitor and reply to reviews in a professional, timely and appropriate way. The aim is for every comment to be treated with respect and for the property's overall image to stay consistent and trustworthy.

Yes. We can help improve your Google Business Profile so that the information about the property or the business is more complete, accurate and better organised.

Reviews and a property's overall online presence affect guests' trust and their decision to book. An up-to-date listing, good presentation and thoughtful replies to reviews all help build a more trustworthy image.

Operations And Care

How we handle cleaning, maintenance, and day-to-day property operations.

Yes. Cleaning and proper preparation of the property are core parts of running a rental. We can coordinate cleaning and turnover schedules so the property is ready for every new arrival.

We help log, communicate and follow up on technical issues. We keep the owner informed about the problem, the steps needed and how it's progressing, so no important issue is left without follow-up.

The cost depends on the type of property, the level of service and how much day-to-day support it needs. When we discuss working together, we explain the fee structure, what's included and which costs may be charged separately.

We can work with you either on a full management package or on specific services, depending on the needs of the property and the owner.
