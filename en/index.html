What Minos Host does

Minos Host

Minos Host is a property management company in Chania focused on short-term rentals, villas and holiday homes. We support owners with the operational side of hosting: guest messaging, check-in coordination, cleaning schedules, maintenance follow-up, pricing support and owner reporting.

We are built for owners who want local presence, professional communication and a property that stays guest-ready throughout the season. Instead of vague promises, we focus on clear responsibilities, responsive support and visible standards.
