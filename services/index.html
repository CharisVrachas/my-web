Property management

We handle the day-to-day operational side of property management, so owners do not have to manage every message, schedule, issue, or follow-up themselves. This includes ongoing coordination, communication, and practical oversight to keep the property functioning well.

Included support may cover

  • Booking and calendar coordination
  • Guest communication before, during and after the stay
  • Check-in and check-out coordination
  • Cleaning and linen scheduling
  • Maintenance reporting and follow-up
  • Owner updates and operational visibility
  • Airbnb account and listing coordination
  • Booking.com account and listing coordination
  • Listing quality and content updates
  • Google Business Profile improvements
  • Review monitoring and responses
  • Ongoing online reputation support

Villa management

Villa management in Chania requires more than basic hosting support. Villas often need closer coordination, stronger presentation standards, more detailed turnover preparation, and faster handling of guest issues or property needs.

Minos Host supports villa owners with dependable local oversight and consistent operational standards. Our goal is to help premium properties stay guest-ready, well cared for, and professionally represented throughout the season.

Short-term rental management

Short-term rental management is about execution. Owners need someone to keep communication timely, turnovers organised, guest expectations clear, and problems addressed before they affect reviews or future bookings.

Minos Host helps owners manage the moving parts of short-term rental operations in Chania with practical support and local coordination. We focus on reliable operations, not empty promises.

Online account management

Minos Host also supports owners with the online side of property management. We can help manage and improve Airbnb and Booking.com accounts, keep listing information aligned, support stronger presentation across platforms, and reduce the time owners spend handling day-to-day digital tasks.

Clear, complete and well-maintained profile information supports trust, and helps platforms and search systems understand the property more accurately.

Guest communication

Guest communication has a direct effect on reviews, satisfaction, and operational calm. Owners want guests to receive clear information, quick replies, and dependable support when something needs attention.

We help manage guest communication across the key points of the stay, including pre-arrival guidance, check-in support, stay assistance, and post-stay follow-up where needed.

Cleaning and turnover coordination

Cleaning coordination is one of the most important parts of successful property operations. A late, inconsistent, or poorly managed turnover can damage guest experience immediately.

Minos Host coordinates cleaning schedules and turnover readiness so properties are prepared for arrivals and maintained to a clear standard.

Maintenance coordination

Properties need small fixes, urgent follow-up, and ongoing care. Owners want to know who notices issues, who reports them, and how quickly they are handled.

We help coordinate maintenance reporting and follow-up, so problems are not ignored and owners are not left chasing updates.

Owner communication and reporting

Owners should not feel disconnected from their property. Good management includes clear communication, regular updates, and practical visibility into what is happening.

Minos Host keeps owners informed through structured communication and straightforward reporting — fees, timing, updates and performance, without having to ask repeatedly.

Review response and online reputation

Online reviews influence how future guests see a property and how confidently they decide to book. We help owners respond to guest reviews in a timely, professional, and consistent way, whether the feedback is positive, mixed, or negative.

We also support the broader reputation side of hosting by helping owners maintain a stronger digital presence across their main channels.
