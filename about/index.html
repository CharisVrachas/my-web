About Minos Host

Minos Host is a property management business focused on helping owners in Chania run villas, holiday homes, and short-term rentals with more confidence and less day-to-day stress. We combine local operational support with clear communication, so owners know their property is being looked after properly.

Our story Our story

Who we are

Our approach is simple: be clear, be responsive, and handle the work that actually affects owner experience and guest satisfaction.

01. About Minos Host

Who we are Local property management

Minos Host was created for property owners who need more than a listing partner. Many owners need a local team that can coordinate the moving parts of hosting, protect standards on the ground, and act responsibly when issues come up.

We do not only focus on physical operations at the property. We also help owners with the digital side of property performance, including booking platform account handling, visibility improvements, and review responses that support a stronger online reputation.

Property management is a trust-based service, so the business should present itself with plain language, visible accountability, and detailed answers to owner questions.

02. What we believe

Our values Organised, transparent, practical

We believe good property management should feel organised, transparent, and practical. Owners should know what services are included, how communication works, how issues are handled, and what level of support they can expect.

We also believe local presence matters. A property in Chania benefits from people who understand the area, can coordinate locally, and can support the owner with real-world follow-through rather than only software or remote messaging.

03. What makes Minos Host different

Our approach A local partner, not a platform

Minos Host is not positioned as a generic “all-in-one platform.” It is positioned as a local property management partner for owners who want practical support, dependable communication, and clarity about how their property is managed.

Minos Host serves owners in Chania with property management support for villas, holiday homes, and short-term rentals. We believe in direct communication, clear responsibilities, and a service model that helps owners stay informed while reducing the operational burden of hosting.
